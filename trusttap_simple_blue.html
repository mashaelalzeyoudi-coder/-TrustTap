Something went wrong?

Choose the situation closest to yours and follow these first steps.

🎣

Suspicious email or link

You received a message that may be phishing.

  1. Do not click links or open attachments.
  2. Verify with the organisation using its official website or a known phone number.
  3. Report the message using your email or service provider's reporting option.
  4. If you entered a password, change it from a trusted device and enable multi-factor authentication.
🏦

Suspicious or fraudulent payment

Money may have been sent to the wrong account.

  1. Contact your bank immediately using its official app or number.
  2. Ask the bank to review the transaction and advise what can be done.
  3. Preserve invoices, messages, timestamps, and transaction references.
  4. Notify the appropriate internal contact and follow official reporting guidance.
🔑

Account may be compromised

Someone may have access to a business account.

  1. Use a trusted device to change the affected password.
  2. Sign out other sessions and enable multi-factor authentication.
  3. Check recovery details, forwarding rules, and recent activity.
  4. Contact the service provider and your IT support, if available.
🛑

Ransomware or suspicious device activity

Files or devices behave unexpectedly.

  1. Disconnect the affected device from networks if safe and practical.
  2. Do not delete evidence or try random tools to fix it.
  3. Contact qualified IT/security support and follow their instructions.
  4. Use clean, known-good backups only with professional guidance.
Important: These are general first steps, not a substitute for professional support. If money or sensitive information is at risk, contact your bank, service provider, or relevant UAE authorities through their official channels as soon as possible.
